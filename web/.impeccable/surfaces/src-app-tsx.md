# Fare web: whole-site surface brief

Scope: the whole site at web/ (Home, App, Developers, Proof). Modes: Home is Persuade (API providers lead, buyers and verifiers each get a door); App is Operate (buyers and agents); Developers and Proof are Read (providers, verifiers). Payment infrastructure, API contract and privacy invariants are unchanged. Build path: code-led (no image generation in this harness). Roll ran degraded (no challengers, impeccable.style unreachable); the owner chose impeccable's pick card over the assigned direction (bearer note).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A pack is a sheet of 100 stamps; every call cancels one with a postmark that names no sender. The site refuses the crypto default (dark, neon, coins, padlocks), its cream-editorial opposite, and the SaaS dashboard of metric cards and sidebars.

OWN-WORLD: Full palette on white gummed sheet paper (cool white, never cream): vermilion for the buyer side and the primary action, ultramarine for the developer and issuer side, postmark blue-black for text and cancellations, stamp green for paid. Dark scheme is a black stamp-album page with stamps mounted on it. Perforated edges, sheet-margin inscriptions (plate number, count, price) in Martian Mono, condensed stamp lettering in Big Shoulders Display, reading text in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Postal terms carry state: postage due (underpaid), already cancelled (replay), returned (unlisted key).

STORY: A provider understands within one viewport that Fare lets their API charge per call and never learn who paid, sees it working (a live 402 from the hosted API, four lines of middleware), and goes to Developers. A buyer goes to App, buys a sheet, spends stamps, watches a replay bounce. A verifier goes to Proof and checks every claim against chain data and the published key.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed vermilion sheet margin, perforated along its lower edge. Top left the two-dot mark and nav set as margin inscription; headline in large white stamp lettering, left, two thirds width: "Charge for your API. Never learn who paid." Beside and below it one oversized white stamp, perforated, whose design is the four-line middleware snippet with a copy button; across its corner a live postmark drawn from GET /v1/ask: 402, 100 calls for 0.001 TAZ, key matches directory. Primary action "Add Fare to your API" (to Developers) at the stamp's foot; secondary "Try it with a demo payment" (to App). Signature interaction: the postmark cancel (ink strike over a stamp), used for each spent token, the replay bounce and the hero's live 402.

BUILD NOTE (finish review, Oct 25): both actions sit under the lede, not at the stamp's foot. The stamp's foot keeps "Copy the example", which belongs to the code; putting the primary action there would make the reading order headline, code, action, and on phones push it below the fold (the layout test holds it in view at 375 px).
